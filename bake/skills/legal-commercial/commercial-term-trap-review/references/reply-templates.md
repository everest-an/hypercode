# Reply Templates

Replace `[Landlord]`, `[Project]`, `[Unit]`, `[Name]`, `[Rate]`, `[Fee]`, `[Deposit]`, `[Date]` and `[Prior version]` before sending.

Keep the reply short. The goal is not to negotiate every point in one email; it is to lock the economic baseline and move the open items into the contract.

---

## 1. Clarify tax-inclusive scope

**Subject:** Clarification on tax-inclusive scope — [Project] / [Unit]

Dear [Name],

Thank you for the term sheet. Before we confirm, please clarify the tax treatment of each line item. Our current reading is:

- Rent: [Rate]% of monthly turnover, plus [X]% VAT on rent.
- Property / service fee: [Fee], stated inclusive of VAT.
- Deposit: [Deposit], stated inclusive of VAT.

Please confirm whether the rent is **tax-inclusive** or **tax-exclusive**. If the rent is tax-inclusive, please confirm that no additional VAT will be charged on the rent.

To avoid ambiguity, we suggest one controlling sentence in the contract:

> "The rent is [Rate]% of monthly turnover, inclusive of VAT; no additional VAT is payable on the rent."

We are keen to move quickly and can proceed once this is confirmed in writing.

Best regards,
[Name]

## 2. Historical precedent

**Subject:** [Project] / [Unit] — tax treatment consistent with prior agreement

Dear [Name],

Thank you for the updated proposal. We reviewed the prior term sheet dated [Prior version]. The prior note stated:

> "Rent, service fee and deposit include VAT."

The current note states:

> "Service fee and deposit include VAT."

The word "rent" has been removed. As the headline rent is unchanged, we read the current version as [Rate]% + [X]% VAT, which is approximately [Effective]% effective.

To keep the commercial baseline consistent, please confirm that the prior treatment applies: **rent at [Rate]%, inclusive of VAT, with no additional VAT on rent**.

If your team has a different interpretation, please share the signed contract or the actual billing basis from the prior period, and we will reconcile against it.

Best regards,
[Name]

## 4. Fallback trade-off

To keep the all-in cost workable, we propose one of the following:

3. A reduction of the deposit to [X] months; or
4. A marketing / promotion fund contribution of [X].

We are flexible on structure and would like to find a solution that works for both sides.

Best regards,
[Name]

## Tone rules

- Thank first; do not accuse.
- Quote the exact words; do not paraphrase a tax clause.
- State your reading; ask for confirmation.
- Offer one controlling sentence, not a legal lecture.
- Keep the door open: "We are keen to move quickly."
- Put the commercial decision in the reply, not in a separate memo.
- Never say "we agree" without the qualifier "subject to written confirmation".
