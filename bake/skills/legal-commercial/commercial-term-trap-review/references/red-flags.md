# Red Flags

Severity:

- **Critical** — do not sign or reply "agree" until resolved in writing.
- **High** — price it, cap it, or trade it before signing.
- **Medium** — clarify in the contract; usually not a deal-breaker.

## Critical

| Flag | Why it matters | Ask for |
|---|---|---|
| Headline rate plus VAT, while a note says "rent inclusive" | Effective cost can be 5–13% higher than the headline | One controlling sentence per line item: inclusive or exclusive, rate, invoice |
| A new version removes a word from an inclusive note ("rent" deleted) | The headline is unchanged; the price is not | Word-level diff; prior contract/bill; written confirmation |
| "含税" with no scope, rate, or invoice type | You cannot verify the price or recover input VAT | Scope, rate, special VAT invoice, timing |
| Free rent says "none" but a note mentions "fit-out rent-free" | You may pay rent during fit-out or lose the fit-out period | One sentence: start, length, fees payable, trigger |
| Landlord may terminate early on short notice with no compensation | You may lose unamortized fit-out and staff costs | Lock-in, compensation formula, reciprocal right |
| Deposit "three months" without a basis | Deposit can be 2–3× higher than expected | Basis (rent/property fee/both), refund period, interest |
| Turnover rent with no turnover definition | The landlord can include returns, VAT, shipping, discounts | Net sales formula, exclusions, audit right |
| Pure percentage "inclusive" but separate property fee applies | The all-in cost is not the headline percentage | Confirm what is included; cap other fees |
| Unilateral right to adjust service/property fee with no cap | The fixed cost can drift | Cap, notice, index, consultation |
| Personal guarantee or unlimited guarantee | Risk extends beyond the project | Cap, duration, entity-only liability |

## High

| Flag | Why it matters | Ask for |
|---|---|---|
| Base rent or turnover rent "whichever is higher" with no crossover analysis | You may pay base rent even when sales are low | Model crossover; cap; break right |
| Marketing/promotion fund with no cap or scope | Hidden occupancy cost | Fixed amount or cap; budget; audit |
| Utilities excluded but no meter or cap | HVAC/water/electricity can be material | Meter, rate, cap, inclusion list |
| Service fee vs property fee with overlapping items | Double charging | One schedule of inclusions; no duplication |
| Deposit applied to "any amount" | Landlord can use it for disputed fees | Apply only to undisputed unpaid rent |
| Restoration/hoarding removal with no cost cap | Exit cost can exceed deposit | Cap, condition report, landlord approval |
| Assignment/change of control restricted | Limits sale or restructuring | Permitted transfer to affiliates; consent not unreasonably withheld |
| Exclusivity/radius clause | Limits expansion or other landlords | Narrow category, geographic radius, remedy |
| Co-tenancy clause with no remedy | Traffic risk without rent relief | Rent reduction or break right if anchor leaves |
| Invoice timing not stated | Cash flow and VAT recovery risk | Invoice within X days of payment |
| Late fee compounding daily | Small delay becomes material | Simple interest, cap, grace period |
| Insurance required but scope/cap unclear | Cost and liability | Limits, named insured, waiver of subrogation |

## Medium

| Flag | Why it matters | Ask for |
|---|---|---|
| Signage/façade approval rights | Brand visibility | Approval timeline; deemed consent |
| Operating hours | Staff cost | Mall hours; right to close on low-traffic days |
| HVAC outside mall hours | Extra cost | Rate, notice, cap |
| POS/data ownership | Customer data | Tenant owns customer data; landlord gets anonymized aggregate |
| Access/loading dock restrictions | Operations | Delivery windows; freight lift |
| Condition report at handover | Dispute at exit | Photo/video condition report signed at handover |
| Language/version precedence | Chinese vs English conflicts | Chinese controls; signed version controls |
| "以出租方确认为准" / "暂定" | No long-stop | Fixed date + long-stop + termination right |
| Subletting/concession rights | Flexibility | Permitted concessions; consent standard |
| Force majeure | Closure risk | Rent abatement; termination after long stop |

## The three-question test

For every red flag, ask:

1. **What is the worst-case cost?** Quantify it in money or percentage of sales.
2. **Who controls the trigger?** If the landlord controls it unilaterally, cap it or add a remedy.
3. **What is the one sentence I need in the contract?** Write it before negotiating.

## Stop conditions

Stop and escalate if:

- the counterparty refuses to confirm tax-inclusive scope in writing;
- the contract contains a personal guarantee or unlimited liability;
- the effective cost is above the approved threshold;
- the landlord can terminate early without compensation and you have unamortized fit-out;
- the version history is missing and the counterparty will not provide the signed baseline.
